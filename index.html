A mechanical engineer turned operator, at home on the shop floor and in the founder's office alike.

Yash Kothari works where design meets manufacturing meets go-to-market. From the founder's office of a fast-scaling consumer-electronics brand, he co-manages design sprints, stands up assembly SOPs, and tests molding methods — including 3D-printed molds — to move products from concept to shelf.

His record runs on hardware. As Chief Manufacturing Officer at DJS Racing he directed the end-to-end build of an electric formula car, and as founder of Hook he designed a clamp-on kit that turns any bicycle electric — a 30% performance boost and 30 km of range.

Earlier, in the founder's office at 26ideas, he authored a team of specialist AI agents and automated business workflows. The method is consistent throughout: organise the chaos, build the system, ship the hardware — then write the SOP so it happens again.
