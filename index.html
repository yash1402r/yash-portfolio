MARGIN NOTE

In My Own Words

// handwritten in the margin

I'm Yash. I'm happiest around problems that end in something you can hold — a car that runs, a prototype that works, a process that didn't exist yesterday.

What I'm genuinely good at is turning chaos into systems: taking a loose idea, giving it structure, and getting a team unstuck so the work actually moves. I learn fastest with a tool in my hand and a deadline in front of me — I'd rather be judged by what I ship than by a transcript.

Hand tools, SolidWorks, or a stack of AI — give me any of them and I'll find the path from concept to running machine. I don't sit still well; I always want the next step. Ownership, clear communication, and a good SOP are how I work. Off the clock, I'm usually learning from builders and operators, or halfway into a side project.

— Yash

Experience

ARMOR · CONSUMER ELECTRONICS
PRESENT

Founder's Office

  • Support founder-level operations across sourcing, product, go-to-market, and production for a fast-scaling brand launching power banks, TWS, adapters, cables, keyboards, mice, and controllers.
  • Own and run the product launch tracking system — coordinating sourcing, BIS/WPC certification, design, packaging, and production timelines across the full portfolio to hold every product to its target launch month.

  • Author the brand's launch & process documentation — factory-vetting guideline, testing sheets, battery-cell sourcing list, multi-stage QC/inspection — in one locked house format.
  • Build GTM market analysis — price-band ladders across 17 categories and a demand × ease-of-entry ranking of 12 accessory lines — to prioritise entry products and bands.
